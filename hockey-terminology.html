Hockey Terminology

Hockey has its own language. This page is where every term a Hornet hears from the bench, the stands, or a coach gets a plain-English definition.

Zones

Defending Zone is where our goalie is. Attacking Zone is where we are trying to score. Neutral Zone is the area between the Defending Zone and the Attacking Zone.

The Slot

The Slot is the high danger area in between the circles, from the goalie's crease up to the top of the circles. We can further subdivide the slot into the Low Slot (area nearest the goalie) and High Slot. This is the highest percentage chance for shots to go in. We want to get pucks to this area when attacking and prevent pucks from getting in this zone when defending. Never pass through this area when we are in our own zone!

Strong Side & Weak Side

Strong Side is the side of the ice where the puck is. Weak Side is the other side (where the puck isn't). Typically you will see more players on the Strong Side of the ice.

Lines & Crease

The Goal Line is the line extending from the goal to the walls. The Red Line is the center line in the middle of the ice. If a player is behind the Red Line and shoots the puck into the Attacking Zone and it goes past the opponent's Goal Line, the team is called for Icing. The referees will bring the puck back to the Defending Zone for a faceoff. The Blue Line is the blue line separating a Defending Zone or Attacking Zone from the Neutral Zone. The puck needs to cross the Blue Line before an attacking player can enter the Attacking Zone — otherwise the team is called for Offsides and there is a faceoff. The Crease, or Blue Paint, is the area around the goal where players other than the goalie are not allowed to go.
